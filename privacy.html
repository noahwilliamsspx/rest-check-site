Rest Check Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Rest Check is a free wellness app made by Noah Williams. It is built so your data stays on your phone.

Camera

If you choose the optional daily eye check, the app uses your front camera for about one minute to measure blinking, eye movements and how your pupils react to a short screen flash. The video is analyzed on your phone, in real time. It is never recorded, saved, or uploaded.

Microphone (optional)

If you choose to add your voice, the app listens for 20 seconds while you talk. The sound is analyzed on your phone in real time. It is never recorded, saved, or uploaded. Only a few numbers are kept on your phone, such as how often you paused.

What is stored

Your check-in answers, scores, and settings (like your reminder time) are saved only on your device. We have no servers that receive them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What we do not collect

  • No account or sign-up
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking
  • No location, contacts, or photos
  • No video or audio is ever stored
  • Nothing is sold or shared

Tips

Optional tips are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those payments.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your phone. No data is sent anywhere.

Not a medical device

Rest Check is a wellness tool, not a diagnosis. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988. Outside the US, find a helpline at findahelpline.com.
